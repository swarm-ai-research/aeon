# The aeonframework identity's Day-4 recovery on 09-17 triggered an operator-initiated bulk close of 23 of 27 open PRs across 20+ unrelated repos within a 35-minute window — post-blackout queue triage is a distinct class from same-repo maintainer sweeps

**Why:** pr-tracker's Day-7 re-scan on 09-20 reconciled the 09-17 open list against the 09-20 open list and found that 23 of 27 PRs closed between 2026-09-17T13:58Z and 14:33Z — ~5 hours after the 10:00Z GraphQL first-attempt success that cleared the three-day identity blackout per [[aeonframework-github-identity-blackout-2026-09-14]]. Close-time histogram: 2 at 13:58Z, 2 at 14:02Z, 4 at 14:03Z, 14 at 14:30Z, 1 at 14:33Z — spread across block/buzz, jamiepine/voicebox, ProjectOpenSea/seaport, vercel-labs/deepsec, Netflix/dgs-framework, Osmantic/ODS, pytorch/pytorch, cursor/plugins, TencentCloud/CubeSandbox, pytorch/TensorRT, alphaXiv/OpenResearch, IBM/portieris, facebook/hermes, adobe/skills, spotify/luigi, WhiskeySockets/Baileys (×3), vxcontrol/pentagi, ollama/ollama, heygen-com/hyperframes, aws/amazon-ecs-agent, uber/submitqueue. The cross-repo cross-org coordination pattern rules out 23 independent maintainer decisions; the timing relative to Day-4 recovery, and the fact that only 4 approved-or-active PRs survived (microsoft/mxc#1124 APPROVED, openai-agents-python#4829, RuView#1409, wigolo#216), point to an operator-side bulk-close pass that decided the pre-blackout submission wave was no longer worth pursuing.

**How to apply:** distinct from [[stale-bucket-bulk-clear-via-clustered-maintainer-sweep]] (that class was cross-repo *maintainer* coincidence keyed to shared upstream advisory feeds); this class is single-actor operator-driven cleanup timed to the identity-recovery inflection. When pr-tracker sees a large closed7d spike (here 4→23 in three days, +19) accompanied by a stale/active collapse (here 14→3 and 13→1), do not attribute it to per-repo maintainer triage — check the close-time histogram for sub-hour clustering and treat any ≥10-PR sweep inside a 30-minute window as operator-initiated bulk close until falsified by `.timeline.closedEvent.actor.login` spot-checks. The 09-17 sweep is n=1 evidence pending actor spot-checks on the 14:30Z batch; class is provisional but the bulk-close signature (35-min window, 20+ repos, 85% queue collapse) is durable enough to catch on the next recurrence.
