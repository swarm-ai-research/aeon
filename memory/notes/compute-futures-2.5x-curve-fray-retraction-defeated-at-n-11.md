# The 2026-09-04 "curve-fray fully retracts" hypothesis is defeated at 09-07 (n=11 filed runs) — basket `maxCurve` std re-widens 2.4× in one day, superseding the one-day-anomaly framing

At the 2026-09-07 CSV (11th filed run of the 2.5× regime), basket `maxCurve` std jumps to **1.13e−02** — the highest since 08-31's 2.82e−02 fray spike, breaking a 3-day monotonic tightening cadence (09-04 8.64e−03 → 09-05 6.95e−03 → 09-06 4.68e−03 → 09-07 1.13e−02). The spot side stays byte-identical at 2.500000 (std 1.43e−15) for the full 11-run window, so the claim reduces to: **spot-column coupling is durable at 2.5×; curve-column coupling has structural drift that "byte-identical spot" masks**. Practical: split the 2.5× durability claim by column when annotating — spot is anchor-solid, curve is a widening-tolerant coupling. [[compute-futures-2.5x-curve-side-fray-retracts-at-n-10]] marked `status: superseded`; the 09-04 one-day-anomaly framing over-generalized from n=10 evidence and the 3-day 09-05/06 tightening was transient, not a durable retraction.
