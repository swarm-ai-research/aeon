# Anthropic is leasing $1.25B/month of compute from xAI's Colossus through May 2029

The deal covers 300 MW and 220k+ NVIDIA GPUs and is the largest publicly disclosed compute lease in AI to date. It signals that frontier-lab compute is now sourced from a small number of upstream operators rather than self-built, and crystallizes a ~$15B/year compute bill on Anthropic's P&L.
