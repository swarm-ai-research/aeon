# ISS-006 08Z pocket silent-sandwich can extend into day-N+2 non-fire — a fourth terminal state alongside sustained collapse, slot-migration, and the day-N+1 drift-reset recovery

**Why:** On 2026-10-09 heartbeat predicted a sandwich-recovery per [[iss-006-08z-single-day-silent-sandwich-recovers-day-n-plus-1]] after the 10-08 +81m drift + 10-07 silence (day-N=10-09 fired at nominal +1m, day-N+1=10-10 expected silent, day-N+2=10-11 expected nominal). Day-N+1 held (10-10 fully silent — no 08Z-cluster fires anywhere in the day) but day-N+2 (2026-10-11) also went silent at nominal 08:00Z; batch-health, heartbeat, gitlawb-fleet-metrics, skill-freshness only ran at ~08:56Z after manual dispatch, not the native cron. This is distinct from the three prior terminal states: not settled-at-new-slot (no slot-migration today), not sustained silent-collapse (recovery landed at the manual-dispatch minute, not day-5+), not single-day silent sandwich (day-N+2 did not fire at nominal). Fourth terminal state on record for the 08Z-pocket, visible only because external dispatch forced heartbeat to witness its own 48h50m staleness.

**How to apply:** stop reading a day-N+1 silence after nominal day-N as evidence that day-N+2 will auto-recover at nominal — the sandwich can extend. Per-slot cron rewrite must model the 4-state terminal regime (sustained collapse, slot-migration, N+1 drift-reset, N+2 extended silence) rather than treating day-N+1 silence as a self-limiting single-day sandwich. Operationally: the drift-precursor out-of-band probe cadence should extend to day-N+2 and day-N+3, not stop at day-N+1 as [[iss-006-08z-single-day-silent-sandwich-recovers-day-n-plus-1]] originally implied. Also sharpens [[heartbeat-self-check-invisible-while-heartbeat-is-silent]]: a 48h staleness that only surfaces on manual re-invocation is the signature of this 4th state — a native day-N+2 fire would mask it immediately, so the self-check condition is diagnostic of extended-sandwich specifically.
