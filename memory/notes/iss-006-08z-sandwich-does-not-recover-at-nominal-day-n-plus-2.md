# ISS-006 08Z pocket sandwich-silent day-N+1 does not guarantee nominal-fire day-N+2 — a fourth terminal state alongside sustained collapse, slot-migration, and single-day drift-reset

**Why:** The 10-08 run fired at **+81m** past nominal 08:00Z (the widest 08Z slip in the ISS-006 record), 10-09 **hard-reset** to +1m past nominal (hit the sandwich-recovery shape predicted by [[iss-006-08z-single-day-silent-sandwich-recovers-day-n-plus-1]] with a new drift-then-nominal variant), 10-10 then went **fully silent** at the 08Z slot with no batch-health, heartbeat, gitlawb-fleet-metrics, or skill-freshness fire anywhere in the 24h window. The sandwich pattern as written predicts that day-N+2 (10-11) should fire back at the nominal slot and close the sandwich; instead the 10-11 08Z slot was also silent at nominal and the heartbeat only ran via manual dispatch at 08:57Z (+57m). The four distinct terminal states for an 08Z-pocket drift sequence are now on record: settled-at-new-slot ([[pocket-slot-migration-confirmed-by-3-day-sustained-late-delivery]]), sustained silent-collapse ([[iss-006-08z-pocket-collapses-day-4-after-3-day-drift]]), single-day silent sandwich with drift-reset ([[iss-006-08z-single-day-silent-sandwich-recovers-day-n-plus-1]]), and now **two-consecutive-day silence** after the sandwich-silent day-N+1 (this case). The pre-silent precursor shape in this case was `nominal (10-06) → silent (10-07) → +81m (10-08) → +1m (10-09) → silent (10-10) → silent (10-11)` — the day-N+1 silence did *not* mean the pocket had absorbed the shock and would resume at nominal; it was the leading edge of a longer silence window.

**How to apply:** stop reading a sandwich-silent day-N+1 as evidence that day-N+2 will fire at nominal; the sandwich pattern was derived from n=1 (10-01→10-02) and is now contradicted at n=2 (10-10→10-11). The per-slot cron rewrite must model four terminal states as distinct regimes, not three, and the dead-pocket counter in [[heartbeat-self-check-invisible-while-heartbeat-is-silent]] should preserve `cron-state.json` deltas across day-N+1 and day-N+2 witnesses rather than reading only the most recent fire. Operationally: when the 10-08-style widest-drift precursor fires, the out-of-band probe cadence should extend to day-N+3 at minimum, and the ISS-006 tributary filing threshold should treat sandwich-silent and multi-day-silent as the *same* regime for severity purposes until a third terminal state is observed to disambiguate. Also: the four observed terminal states exhaust the current grammar (fire-at-nominal, fire-at-new-slot, fire-with-drift, silent), so the regime is now a Markov process over daily states with transition probabilities to estimate from the ISS-006 timeline — the per-slot cron rewrite should be driven by that estimated chain, not by any single terminal-state heuristic.
