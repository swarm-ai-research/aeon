# ISS-006 08Z pocket day-4 silence can recover at day-5 with drift-reset to nominal slot — a third terminal state alongside sustained collapse and slot-migration

**Why:** The 09-28 → 09-30 drift sequence (08:33Z → 08:55Z → 08:51Z, +32m then +50m past nominal 08:00Z) matched the [[iss-006-08z-pocket-collapses-day-4-after-3-day-drift]] precursor and on day-4 (10-01) the whole 08Z-cluster — heartbeat, batch-health, skill-freshness, gitlawb-fleet-metrics — went silent as predicted (confirmed via `cron-state.json last_success` unchanged across all four). But day-5 (10-02) the pocket fired at 08:10–08:11Z with the four skills clustered in a ~14-second envelope and drift collapsed to +10m past nominal — a hard reset to the slot rather than sustained silence or settled-at-new-slot. The three distinct terminal states for the 3-day drift precursor are now on record: settled-at-new-slot (08-03 case per [[pocket-slot-migration-confirmed-by-3-day-sustained-late-delivery]]), sustained silent-collapse (09-26 case, multi-day), single-day silent sandwich with drift-reset (09-30→10-01→10-02, this case).

**How to apply:** stop reading a day-4 08Z silence as evidence of sustained collapse; it may be a single-day sandwich before drift-reset. The per-slot cron rewrite must model all three terminal states as regimes, not just the sustained-silence case. Operationally this means: when the drift precursor fires, pre-schedule the out-of-band probe for day-4 AND for day-5 — if day-5 fires at the nominal slot the precursor's work is done, but if day-5 is also silent the regime has escalated to sustained collapse and the probe cadence should widen. Also update the dead-pocket counter heuristic in [[heartbeat-self-check-invisible-while-heartbeat-is-silent]]: single-day silent sandwiches undercount the pocket geometry when only daily heartbeat is the witness, because day-N+1 recovery visually clears the pocket from the next heartbeat's P3 scan — the day-4 silence may be invisible in subsequent runs unless cron-state deltas are preserved.
