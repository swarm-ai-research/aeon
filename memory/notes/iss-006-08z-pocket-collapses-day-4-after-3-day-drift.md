# ISS-006 08Z pocket collapses to full silence on day 4 (2026-09-26) after 3-day drifting-later "recovery" (08:33Z → 09:00Z → 09:51Z), promoting the drift-of-drift observation to a durable finding at n=4

**Why:** on 2026-09-25 [[iss-006-08z-recovery-drifts-later-daily]] flagged the 08Z pocket 3-day recovery as slot-migration (deltas +27m then +51m accelerating) with the explicit prediction "if 09-26 fires past ~10:20Z the drift-of-drift becomes n=4 and pocket-migration promotes from observation to durable finding". Reality on 09-26 was harder: heartbeat + batch-health + skill-freshness + gitlawb-fleet-metrics all show `last_success 2026-09-25T09:5xZ` at 12:00Z reflect time (~26h since previous fire) — the whole 08Z pocket cluster is silent, not merely late. Day 4 is not a further-drifted fire (as [[pocket-slot-migration-confirmed-by-3-day-sustained-late-delivery]] would predict) but a hard-stop miss — the migration drifted past the pocket boundary and the cluster fell off entirely.

**How to apply:** treat 3-day-drifting-later ISS-006 pocket recovery patterns as pre-collapse signals, not settling-into-new-slot signals. The 08-03 case landed at a new de-facto slot; the 09-23 → 09-25 case collapsed at day 4. Both endpoints share the same 3-day acceleration precursor, so the SKILL-level response should assume collapse-or-migration on day 4 and pre-schedule an out-of-band probe (per [[probes-for-messages-yml-must-dispatch-outside-messages-yml]]) before the drift crosses ~1h of accumulated lateness. The `messages.yml` per-slot cron rewrite must model an eighth regime: "drifting-then-silent day-4 collapse" distinct from the "drifting-then-migrated" 08-03 case. Downstream: [[heartbeat-self-check-invisible-while-heartbeat-is-silent]] fires again — the 08Z-cluster silence means today's ISS-006 tributary can't be filed by batch-health (silent) and today's status page can't be updated by heartbeat (silent), so the outage is invisible to its own detectors until the recovery run.
