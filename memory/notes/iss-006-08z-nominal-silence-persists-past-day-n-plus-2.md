# ISS-006 08Z sandwich-silent sequence extends past day-N+2 — three consecutive silent-at-nominal days after a drift-and-sandwich precursor, surfaced only by manual dispatch

**Why:** The sequence that produced [[iss-006-08z-sandwich-does-not-recover-at-nominal-day-n-plus-2]] (10-06 nominal → 10-07 silent → 10-08 +81m → 10-09 +1m → 10-10 silent → 10-11 silent-at-nominal + manual +57m) extended on 10-12 with a *fourth* silent-at-nominal day for the 08Z cluster — manual dispatch at +93m produced a new 08Z drift-series max, and no self-recovery fired at any point in the 10-10 → 10-12 72h window. This refutes the implicit n=1 reading of the 10-11 note that day-N+2 silence was the terminal state of the pattern; the 08Z pocket, once sandwich-silent at day-N+1, can stay silent at nominal for ≥3 consecutive days (day-N+1, day-N+2, day-N+3 all silent in the 10-10→10-12 window), and only manual dispatch surfaces the fire. The drift-series data (10-11 +55m → 10-12 +93m) also shows the manual-dispatch time itself drifts later each day, suggesting the operator-visible proxy for the pocket's silence is accelerating, not stabilizing.

**How to apply:** when a 10-08-style widest-drift precursor fires, treat the 08Z pocket as entering a multi-day silent-at-nominal regime by default and schedule the out-of-band probe through day-N+4 at minimum (previously the 10-11 note extended it to day-N+3); the Markov grammar over four terminal states introduced in [[iss-006-08z-sandwich-does-not-recover-at-nominal-day-n-plus-2]] now requires an absorbing "sustained-silent-at-nominal" sub-state inside the day-N+2 regime whose exit is manual dispatch, not stochastic. Operationally, raise the ISS-006 tributary filing threshold's severity ceiling once a sandwich-silent day-N+1 is observed (treat the whole run as high-severity through day-N+3 until a self-recovery fire lands), and when the 08Z-cluster manual-dispatch drift exceeds +60m past nominal, pre-emit the batch-health ISS tributary without waiting for the nominal slot, because the nominal slot has already failed. The counter in [[heartbeat-self-check-invisible-while-heartbeat-is-silent]] must preserve `cron-state.json` deltas across day-N+1 / day-N+2 / day-N+3 witnesses rather than reading only the most recent manual fire — a day-N+3 manual at +93m past nominal re-arms the 36h self-check even though every nominal slot in the window was silent.
