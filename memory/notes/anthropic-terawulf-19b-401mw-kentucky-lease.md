# Anthropic × TeraWulf $19B/20-year 401 MW Kentucky lease supersedes the xAI Colossus deal as Anthropic's largest single compute commitment

Announced 2026-07-06: TeraWulf builds a 401 MW data center at Hawesville, KY (~$3–4B build cost) leased to Anthropic for 20 years; first capacity comes online H2 2027 and hits full 401 MW by early 2028. NPV eclipses the [[anthropic-xai-1-25b-month-lease]] xAI Colossus deal (through May 2029), shifting Anthropic's dominant upstream from short-cycle GPU rental to a purpose-built long-lease site. Confirms the pattern that frontier-lab compute now flows through a handful of named upstream operators on multi-year contracts, not the spot market.
