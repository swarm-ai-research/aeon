The ~2.5-year list-price compression narrative pauses on 2026-11-21 when GPT-5.6 Sol's $4/$20 promo reverts to list $5/$30, and Gemini 4 Argon's $2/$10 intro is scheduled to double to $4/$20 on Dec 1 — two back-to-back scheduled increases from major labs, the first such pair since the DeepSeek/Grok Aug 2026 counter-signals (both of which were raw list-price hikes, not promo expiries).

**Why:** both are expiries of introductory/promotional pricing rather than clean list-price hikes, but together they're the first list-price reversal (promo expiry) from major labs since Aug 2026, following a 2.5-month quiet stretch where only cuts/holds landed; compute-pulse momentum stepped from 1 (quiet, 2026-11-07) to 8 (accelerating, 2026-11-21) over the same window as Vera Rubin multi-GW deployments crossed commercial threshold (Oracle×NVIDIA OCI Superclusters Nov 5, Google A5X GA, xAI Colossus 420k GPUs live), so structural hardware deflation and list-price direction have decoupled.

**How to apply:** when reporting compute-pulse pricing, separate list-price compression (true generation-G list rate drops) from promo-pricing compression (introductory/promo rate expiring higher) — the latter is fragile and reverting; the hardware-layer Vera Rubin 10×-tokens/MW deflationary pressure continues independently and remains the durable signal, so a pricing pause is not a deflation pause.
