# ISS-006 08Z-cohort day-N+2+ silent-at-nominal regime converges, after a 4-day alternating silent/manual-dispatch window, into a consecutive-manual-dispatch sub-regime whose drift settles at a new mid-morning slot

**Why:** The 10-29 → 11-04 seven-day 08Z-cohort silent-at-nominal sequence extends the n=4 alternating variant from [[iss-006-08z-cohort-silent-alternates-with-manual-dispatch]] through 11-04: 10-29 silent / 10-30 manual +60m / 10-31 silent / 11-01 manual +40m / 11-02 manual +118m / 11-03 manual +110m / 11-04 manual +111m, so after n=4 alternating days (10-29→11-01) the regime transitioned to three consecutive-manual-dispatch days (11-02/11-03/11-04) with manual drift settling into a ±5m jitter cluster near +110m past nominal — effectively a new dispatch slot at ~09:50Z. This is a *third* sub-shape for the day-N+2+ regime, distinct from the 10-10→10-12 continuous-manual-with-accelerating-drift shape (+55m → +93m) and the 10-29→11-01 alternating-with-decelerating-drift shape (+60m → +40m); here the drift reverses direction once (+40m → +118m) then stabilizes, matching the sustained-late-delivery slot-migration terminal from [[pocket-slot-migration-confirmed-by-3-day-sustained-late-delivery]] but with an alternating-regime precursor rather than a drifting-later precursor.

**How to apply:** when the alternating silent/manual sub-regime from [[iss-006-08z-cohort-silent-alternates-with-manual-dispatch]] is witnessed at n≥3, treat an abrupt drift jump (e.g. +40m → +118m) as the transition signal into the consecutive-manual settled-slot sub-regime, not an outlier — the pocket has entered slot-migration-with-operator-coupling, where the nominal 08Z slot is dead and the operator's manual-dispatch cadence becomes the de facto schedule. For the Markov grammar over ISS-006 terminal states, add this as a composite terminal: `alternating-silent/manual (n≥3) → abrupt-drift-reversal → consecutive-manual with ±jitter at new slot`, and treat the ~110m attractor as an empirical calibration point for where the post-alternating slot settles. Post-settlement the heartbeat self-check breach from [[heartbeat-self-check-invisible-while-heartbeat-is-silent]] clears every day because manual dispatches now anchor `last_success` within 24h, but the underlying nominal-slot dispatch rate remains 0 — the 36h breach threshold is a false-green under this sub-regime, so pair it with a separate "nominal-slot-fired-today" counter that stays independent of manual-dispatch success.
