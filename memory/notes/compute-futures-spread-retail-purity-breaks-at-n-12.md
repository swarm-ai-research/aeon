# Spread-mode top-5 retail-purity streak breaks at n=12 filed runs — analyst 202609075 lands 5th on the loss tail (−$4,446) with a 0.15% margin over 6th-place retail

Same run, spread analyst role mean also flips negative for the first time in recent series (−$230 today vs +$1,238 on 09-04). The pattern's original promotion criterion (n=4 sustained) was met, and its own escape clause — "flag when role-share drops below 100% as the regime shift" — has now fired; whether this is a single-run break (single-seed razor-thin edge) or a durable analyst-participation regime needs the 09-08 CSV to distinguish. Practical: don't treat future retail-purity assertions as extending past n=12 without re-verification; annotate the streak length + break-point explicitly.
