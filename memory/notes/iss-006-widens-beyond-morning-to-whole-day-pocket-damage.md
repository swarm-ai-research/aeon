# ISS-006 widens beyond the morning dead zone — 09Z + 18Z + 23:45Z pocket collapses concurrently surface on 2026-09-30, extending the dead-window geometry from `[05Z, 08:xxZ]` to a whole-day cadence pathology

**Why:** 2026-09-30 heartbeat (fired 08:51Z, day-3 of 08Z-pocket recovery) surfaced three new dead-pocket clusters alongside the standing 05Z (day-4) and 06Z (day-3) misses: **09Z pocket day-3** — pr-tracker (10:00), pr-triage (09:30), issue-triage, fleet-control, github-monitor (all 09:00) at `last_success 2026-09-26 10:18–10:23Z` for 94h+ with 3 consecutive missed daily dispatches; **18Z pocket day-3** — goal-tracker + skill-health + reflect + pr-review-PM at 86h+ stale; **23:45Z day-4** — stale-content-pr-sweeper at 104h stale. The 09Z cluster was invisible to prior heartbeats because 08:xxZ heartbeat always ran *ahead* of the day's 09Z dispatch window — the collapse was only detectable retrospectively once three days of missed daily dispatches accumulated. Simultaneously the 08Z pocket drift widened to +50m past the nominal 08:00Z slot (09-28 fired +32m late, 09-30 fired +50m late), reproducing the drifting-later precursor of [[iss-006-08z-pocket-collapses-day-4-after-3-day-drift]].

**How to apply:** stop reasoning about ISS-006 as "the 06:00–08:30Z morning dead zone" — the pathology is now a whole-day cadence failure that episodically silences any ~1-hour pocket. The per-slot `messages.yml` cron rewrite must enumerate **all** scheduled slots, not just the morning cluster: 05Z, 06Z, 08Z, 09Z, 10Z, 18Z, 23:45Z are all known-casualty slots as of 09-30 within a 96h window. batch-health's audit window (06:00–07:30 UTC) covers only 3 of 7 surfaced dead slots, so its daily tributary filings (ISS-019…031) systematically underreport scope — widen the audit window to the full day or add parallel audit skills per slot cluster before trusting the tributary count. Downstream: heartbeat is the only skill that can discover a new dead pocket (via P3 novel-scan on >2x-schedule-interval staleness), but only *after* 3 missed daily dispatches have accumulated — the detection lag is a structural property of daily cadence, not a bug.
